DECIDE FROM OBSERVED CHANGE
For every decision, including Y/Z corrections:
1. CURRENT: Identify the button relative to the marked contact in each usable
   view. Distinguish visible, cropped and unknown. HEAD gives global alignment;
   WRIST gives local geometry. Do not invent a missing view's geometry.
2. FEEDBACK: Compare views before/after the last actually executed command.
   Did visibility and alignment improve, worsen, stay unchanged or become unknown?
   Sending finished does not prove arrival, contact or progress. On the first turn
   say no prior action; a rejected proposal was not executed, and re-observation
   alone does not move anything. Head/gripper changes can invalidate a comparison.
3. NEXT: Choose from that evidence. If the last direction worsened alignment or
   lost the target, reassess it or improve the view. Repetition without improvement
   needs new evidence, not the same purpose sentence. A button exiting the wrist
   image through the top after lowering is evidence AGAINST lowering to reveal it.
   Do not blindly reverse either; use current views and actual effects.
